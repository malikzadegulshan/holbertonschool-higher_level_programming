------------------------------
Basic usage
------------------------------

Text ending with a period:
    >>> text_indentation("Hello.")
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>

Text ending with a question mark:
    >>> text_indentation("How are you?")
    How are you?
    <BLANKLINE>

Text ending with a colon:
    >>> text_indentation("The following:")
    The following:
    <BLANKLINE>

Text with multiple delimiters:
    >>> text_indentation("Hello. How are you? Good:")
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>
    How are you?
    <BLANKLINE>
    Good:
    <BLANKLINE>

Text with no delimiter (no extra newlines, no trailing newline):
    >>> text_indentation("Hello there")
    Hello there

Text ending without a delimiter (trailing text printed without newline):
    >>> text_indentation("Hello. World")
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>
    World

------------------------------
Spaces handling
------------------------------

Leading spaces after a delimiter are stripped:
    >>> text_indentation("Hello.   next")
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>
    next

Leading and trailing spaces in the whole text are stripped per line:
    >>> text_indentation("   Hello.   ")
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>

Spaces around a delimiter:
    >>> text_indentation("  Hello  .  World  ")
    Hello  .
    <BLANKLINE>
    World

------------------------------
Edge cases
------------------------------

Empty string (prints nothing):
    >>> text_indentation("")

Only a delimiter:
    >>> text_indentation(".")
    .
    <BLANKLINE>

Multiple consecutive delimiters:
    >>> text_indentation("?:.")
    ?
    <BLANKLINE>
    :
    <BLANKLINE>
    .
    <BLANKLINE>

All three delimiters in one sentence:
    >>> text_indentation("Wait: really? Yes.")
    Wait:
    <BLANKLINE>
    really?
    <BLANKLINE>
    Yes.
    <BLANKLINE>
